through 0x7E, 0x7D, 0x7C, 0x7B. With currentCommand in 0x7C the block
brings one new interferer and is refused (2*15 < 30+1); 0x7B's block
(the piece's only in-loop reference, the store through a0) is then
accepted at 2*16 >= 31. Inline, 0x7C brings no new interferer and is
accepted (left 17 to 16), so 0x7B fails at 2*15 < 31 and the piece has no
reference and is dropped. The target's 0x7C is the inline form and still
has the piece, so the target's numintf at that growth is at most 29 or its
0x7C block removes three registers.
