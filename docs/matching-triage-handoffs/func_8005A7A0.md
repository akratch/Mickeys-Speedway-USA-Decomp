#### Owned pass, 2026-09-10 (lane/nm-resident2)

Still 10. What this pass adds is a decomposition of the residual and a
calibration of the frame, not a match.

The ten words are three independent facts:

- **Two words are the frame**, 0x38 against 0x50, at the prologue and the
  epilogue. Both sides address *identical* stack slots -- the same six
  saved-register homes and the same single spill home. The 24-byte difference
  is unaddressed space above them: the target reserves two carrier words there
  and the candidate reserves eight.
- **Four words are one colour.** The `firstAnimation & 3` value lives in a
  callee-saved register in the target and in a caller-saved one here, which
  changes the register at its definition, at its test, at its spill and at the
  reload's consumer.
- **Four words are one schedule**, the argument load and the spill exchanging
  places across the second call, which follows from the colour above: a
  callee-saved carrier does not need to be spilled before the first call.

**Frame calibration (new).** Each additional *live scalar carrier* costs four
bytes, rounded up to eight: adding two used locals moves the frame 0x50 ->
0x58 with the score unchanged at 10. An *unused* declared local is frame-inert
-- it never becomes a carrier. And removing a used local does not necessarily
remove a carrier: dropping `lastAnimation` into its single expression left the
frame at 0x50 and regressed the score to 27, because the expression still
needs the same home.

So the routed lever `drop-a-declared-local` is not sufficient as stated. The
requirement is **six fewer live scalar carriers at the same 106 words**, which
declaration count only sometimes controls; the earlier pass's 0x48 and 0x30
frames are the two- and eight-carrier points either side of the target's two.

##### The frame half: 24 bytes is six declarations, and the rule was fitted

Adding unused `s32` locals to this function moves the frame, which is the
opposite of what L99 says about an unused `s32` and is exactly what L121 says
about a declared local reserving a home. Seven points were measured -- 6, 7, 8,
9, 10, 12 and 16 declarations giving 0x50, 0x50, 0x58, 0x58, 0x60, 0x68 and 0x78
-- and one rule fits all seven:

    frame = round8(52 + 4 * declarations)

The target's 0x38 therefore requires **at most one declared local**, and our six
cost exactly the 24 bytes of the gap. Three further facts pin it:

- **Sibling scopes do not share a home.** Rewriting the body so that
  `lastAnimation`, `loadSize` and the two loop counters live in three disjoint
  nested blocks is **byte-identical** at 10 words and leaves the frame at 0x50.
  Scope is not a lever on this count; declaration is.
- **Parameters cost nothing.** Moving the six locals into trailing parameters of
  the prototype does reach frame 0x38 -- five or six of them are enough -- which
  confirms the arithmetic from the other side, but the entry code changes and it
  scores 102 at delta +8.
- **It is not a whole-TU property.** Two synthetic functions with a loop, a call
  and six locals, compiled *inside this translation unit at its configured
  flags*, charge nothing for four extra unused locals, and neither does a twelve
  local function that genuinely spills. `func_8005A948` and `func_8005AAC0` in
  this TU do charge, and `func_8005AAC0` is a matched function, so the homing is
  real in the shipped build and not a harness artefact.

**Declaration merging does not reach it.** All 203 set partitions of the six
locals, crossed with hosting any one block in the `modelId` parameter, is 877
measured candidates. The incumbent six-declaration form is the **unique**
optimum at 10; every merge costs at least 2. Frame 0x38 appears in 19 of the 877
and the best of them is 88 words, because every one of them merges `loaded` with
`inputOffset`, which are simultaneously live -- so those cells are not legal
reconstructions at all, they are semantically wrong forms that happen to score.
No legal merge reaches 0x38: the legal floor is three declarations, and three
declarations lands at 0x30, not 0x38, because the temp term moves with the code.

**One statement carries the whole 24 bytes.** Making the load size
unconditional -- deleting `if (alignment != 0) { loadSize += 8; }` -- takes the
frame from 0x50 to 0x38 and makes unused locals free again, at a cost of one
instruction. Every form of that conditional that keeps the instruction keeps the
homes: both arms assigning, the ternary at the call argument, the ternary as an
addend, a boolean shifted, a boolean multiplied, a second local for the adjusted
size, and the truth-test and reversed spellings. So the conditional is necessary
and the homes come with it; what the target must differ in is the number of
*declarations* standing beside it.

is refuted before it is spent. The frame arithmetic `round8(52 + 4N)` still
fits the incumbent body's seven measured points; it is the interpretation of N
as a class count that fails.

**The index idiom, which closed `func_8003A5A0` the same day, does not apply
here.** Rewriting the two loops to walk by subscript and letting uopt
strength-reduce -- the animation array indexed by the loaded counter, the input
table indexed by the alignment plus the counter -- costs 8 bytes and scores 96,
in all three decompositions: both loops converted, the outer only, and the inner
only (the inner-only form is delta 0 at 15). The reason is visible and is worth
recording, because it also justifies the incumbent's odd spelling: the animation
pointer is a STRUCT FIELD that each callee could change, so uopt must reload it
every iteration and cannot create an induction pointer from it. The explicit
byte offsets in the retained body are therefore already the strength-reduced
form, and there is nothing for the compiler to derive.

Also measured and worse, all with the conditional kept: the conditional's test
written inline against the first animation rather than through the alignment
local (byte-identical, frame 0x50); both arms assigning the load size (delta
+12, frame 0x50); and a zero-initialised adjustment added afterwards (delta +4,
frame 0x50).

**Reopen condition, restated.** Unchanged on the colour half. On the frame half
it is now: find what makes ugen reserve the local block for a procedure whose
locals it has coloured. It is not the storage class, it is not the declaration
count alone, and it is switched by a single conditional worth one instruction.
