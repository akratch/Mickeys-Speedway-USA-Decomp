#### Frame identity: proved, and the earlier reading corrected

Under this unit's `-O2 -g3`, **every declared local takes a 4-byte home**, in
declaration order, counted down from the top of the local block, whether or not
it is ever reached through the stack. Proved twice: adding one unused `s32`
before the first declaration grew the frame by 8 and moved every home by 4; and
a whole-list permutation that hoisted six trailing declarations to the head put
every home at the offset the law predicts, including the two array bases.

The frame decomposes as 40 bytes of outgoing arguments, 8 bytes of saved
registers (only two registers are saved on either side), the declared local
block, and the ugen spill temps, rounded to 8:

- target: 40 + 8 + 216 + 16 = 280
- candidate: 40 + 8 + 216 + 8 = 272

**Both sides declare exactly 54 local words.** The 8-byte gap is two extra ugen
spill temp words in the target, allocated below the declared block; the target
uses four temp words and the candidate two. This is visible in the reuse
pattern -- a temp slot homes several unrelated registers inside one block,
which no declared local does -- and in the two sides using the same temp index
counted down from the top of the temp region for the same code.

That corrects the previous packet's routing. The 8 bytes are **not** recoverable
by editing the declaration list, and two ways of trying were measured and
rejected:

- Adding a 55th declared local reaches 0x118 only through the 8-byte rounding.
  It displaces every home by 4 and buys 2 words (1422 -> 1420). Not the fix.
- The whole-list permutation reproduced the predicted homes exactly and left
  the score at 1422, flat. Declaration order is measurable here and, on its
  own, inert -- matching the caution measured on another overlay unit the same
  day.

The declaration-order fact that *is* proved, from relative geometry alone: the
